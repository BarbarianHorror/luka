About the Lab

Located in the heart of the UCLA campus, the Language Processing Lab is a vibrant community of faculty, postdocs, graduate students, and undergraduates dedicated to the study of psycholinguistics.

Our research investigates a wide range of topics, including syntactic parsing, lexical access, prosody, and bilingual language processing. We employ various methodologies, from traditional self-paced reading and eye-tracking to computational modeling, to uncover the architecture of the human language faculty.

Current Projects

Explore the ongoing investigations currently being conducted by our researchers and students.

Real-time Sentence Processing

Investigating how comprehenders resolve syntactic ambiguities on the fly using eye-tracking while reading and auditory listening paradigms.

Read more

Bilingual Lexical Access

Examining cross-linguistic activation in early and late bilinguals to understand the organization of the bilingual mental lexicon.

Read more

Computational Psycholinguistics

Utilizing information-theoretic models (like Surprisal Theory) and Large Language Models to predict human reading times and behavioral responses.
